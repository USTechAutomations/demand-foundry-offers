How this works

Pick the one that matches a problem you actually have and use its button. That opens our contact form with the offer attached, so we know which one you mean. We reply with the exact scope in writing. Nothing is billed until you agree to that scope in writing, and if we cannot build it well we say so and you pay nothing.

If your problem is close to one of these but not the same, say so on the form. Adjacent problems are the useful ones.

Why the page is written this way

We would rather publish an honest offer and find out nobody wants it than build software first and discover the same thing later at greater expense. Each offer above is a hypothesis about a real bottleneck. Publishing it with a price attached is the cheapest way we know to test it.

Ordering note: these are listed by how much related search language our own site was matched against over 90 days (2026-05-06 to 2026-08-03: 57,859 distinct queries, 922,006 impressions, 327 clicks). That ordering is a heuristic about topic, not evidence that anyone wants to buy — a 0.0355% click-through rate is machine-shaped volume. It is why this page is a test rather than a catalogue.
