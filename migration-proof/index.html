What the comparison covered

The synthetic comparison covers optional filters, empty reports, month boundaries, aggregate joins, ordered running totals and large decimals: 3 synthetic procedures and 11 matched cases. It did not assess a customer estate. A customer migration assessment is a separate piece of work.
